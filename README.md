# StoriPicha

**Video Intelligence for modern streaming platforms.**

StoriPicha builds tools that help video teams understand how viewers experience their streaming products.

Our goal is simple:

> **Turn video playback telemetry into actionable intelligence.**

## StoriPicha Insights

**StoriPicha Insights** is a video intelligence and observability platform for OTT, live streaming, and video-on-demand products.

It transforms playback telemetry into insights across:

- Playback performance
- Quality of Experience (QoE)
- Buffering and startup performance
- Playback errors
- Viewer engagement
- Content performance
- Devices and platforms
- Networks and delivery infrastructure

```text
Video Player
     ↓
StoriPicha SDK
     ↓
Telemetry
     ↓
Playback Sessions
     ↓
Video Intelligence
```

## What We're Building

StoriPicha Insights is being built around three core areas:

**Observe**  
Understand what happens during every playback session.

**Measure**  
Track playback quality, engagement, failures, and performance.

**Understand**  
Turn streaming data into intelligence that engineering and product teams can act on.

## SDK-First

StoriPicha is designed to integrate directly with existing video players through lightweight SDKs and player adapters.

Initial development is focused on Android and Media3, with additional platforms planned as the ecosystem grows.

## Technology

Our platform is being built with technologies including:

`Kotlin` · `Ktor` · `PostgreSQL` · `Prometheus` · `Android` · `Media3`
